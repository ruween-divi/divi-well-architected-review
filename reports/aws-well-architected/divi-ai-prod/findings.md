**Description**
The 1,000 GiB gp3 volume has about 181 GiB (195 GB) free — 82% used — and storage autoscaling is not configured (no MaxAllocatedStorage), so the
database cannot grow by itself. Since the initial load in mid-August free space has fallen by about 0.9 GB a day, with steps from manual loads: on
6 Nov it dropped 42 GB in one day, the day 38.5 GB left the bastion host. A CDC re-snapshot (AWS-WA-103) would need far more than what is left.
No alarm watches free storage (AWS-WA-108). The staging database, which runs the same code, was storage-full for four days in August.

**Evidence**
- AllocatedStorage 1000 GiB, gp3 (12,000 IOPS / 500 MiB/s), MaxAllocatedStorage not set, status available [Confirmed · rds describe-db-instances · 2026-11-12]
- FreeStorageSpace daily minimum: 988 GB (2026-08-13) → 287 GB (08-20, initial load) → 219.2 GB (11-05) → 177.4 GB (11-06) → 173.4 GB (11-11) → 194.8 GB (11-12, partial day) [Measured · AWS/RDS · 1-day period · 90 days]
- Underlying decline ≈ 0.86 GB/day (08-21 → 11-05), ≈ 7 months to full; single-day drop of 41.8 GB on 2026-11-06 [Estimated from the series]
- divi-prod-bastion NetworkOut 38.5 GB on 2026-11-06 (no other day above 0.1 GB in 90 days) — a manual data load [Measured · AWS/EC2; Inferred]
- No CloudWatch alarm on FreeStorageSpace (18 alarms reviewed) [Confirmed · cloudwatch describe-alarms]
- About 703 GB of the ~879 GB now used was written by the CDC initial load on 14–17 Aug 2026 (free space 988 → 285 GB while the sink scaled to 19 tasks and RDS write throughput averaged 66 MB/s on 16 Aug) [Measured; attribution Inferred · see AWS-WA-129 and the deep dive]
- Most of that space is two sink log tables in the nocelle database: `source_sync.cdc_audit_log` 325.8 GB and `source_sync.sync_log` 241.4 GB. Sizes are heap only, from PostgreSQL's autovacuum log (last logged 17 Aug). Truncating them or giving them a retention period, after owner agreement, would free roughly 570 GB without any storage change [Measured · RDS PostgreSQL log in CloudWatch]
- Other large tables: `public.fact_daily_product_forecast` 33.7 GB and `public.recommendation_model1` 32.0 GB (nocelle), 16.0 GB (intrim). Leftover copies and backup schemas also remain, such as `*_copy1`–`_copy3` in nocelle `public`, and `source_sync_old` and `source_sync_bkp*` in intrim [Measured · same source]

**Business impact**
A storage-full database stops writes for every tenant, API and pipeline at once; recovery needs a storage modification that can take hours.

**Technical impact**
RDS moves the instance to storage-full and shuts the database down; storage modifications are rate-limited and followed by storage optimisation.
A new tenant backfill (intrim, AWS-WA-102) or CDC catch-up (AWS-WA-103) would consume the remaining headroom much faster.

**Recommended remediation**
1. Set MaxAllocatedStorage (for example 1,500–2,000 GiB). There is no cost until storage is actually used.
2. Alarm on FreeStorageSpace (for example below 20% and below 10% of allocated) and subscribe to the RDS low-storage events (AWS-WA-108).
3. Find what is growing (table/index sizes, dead tuples, staging/landing tables, log tables) with Performance Insights and `pg_total_relation_size`; agree retention.
4. Verify: MaxAllocatedStorage above AllocatedStorage; alarm in OK state; growth rate written down.
*Do not / alternatives considered:* a large one-off increase is not needed first; allocated storage can never be reduced again.

**Implementation complexity:** Low (one setting and two alarms in Terraform; no downtime)
**Trade-offs / risks:** autoscaled storage cannot shrink; cost grows with data ($0.138/GB-month single-AZ, Sydney list price).

**Cost impact**
None until storage grows; each additional 100 GB ≈ $13.80/month single-AZ [Estimated · AWS Pricing API 2026-11-12].

**References**
- https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonRDS/latest/UserGuide/USER_PIOPS.Autoscaling.html — autoscaling needs a maximum storage threshold above allocated storage
- https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonRDS/latest/UserGuide/USER_Events.Messages.html — low-storage and storage-full RDS events
